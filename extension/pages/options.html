Glass sizes

The default size is what your "log" shortcuts record. In the popup, press 1–9 to log a specific size.

Browser shortcuts

These work on every tab, including new-tab and settings pages. Browsers require them to include Ctrl or Alt, and you change them on the browser's own shortcuts page.

In-page shortcuts

Any key you like, such as Shift+/. They work on normal websites, but not on browser pages like the new-tab page. They're ignored while you're typing in a text field.
